BingeBox v23.2

PAYPAL
- PayPal support now uses PayPal's hosted-button form endpoint directly.
- Clicking Continue with PayPal opens PayPal in a separate browser tab.
- Removes the embedded PayPal SDK rendering path from the support dialog.
- PayMongo continues to open in a separate tab.

WORKSPACE RIGHTS
- Added Auto-confirm permission in Workspace.
- Enabled by default in the administrator's browser.
- New or currently unverified dramas open with publishing permission checked.
- Saving the drama records the normal drama_rights verification record.
- The setting is visible and can be turned off at any time.
- Existing verified rights records remain unchanged.

- Cleaner Workspace drama library.
- v23.0 international SEO, PayMongo, dynamic drama SEO, player, notification and rights workflows.
