BingeBox v23.9 — Clean Premium Catalog Redesign

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Reworked the homepage around the clean, catalog-first information architecture common to premium short-drama streaming services.
- No ReelShort assets, logos, code, or proprietary copy are included. BingeBox branding and product behavior remain original.

HOMEPAGE
- Replaced the oversized editorial hero with a focused 5-item featured carousel.
- Hero includes title, genre, episode count, concise description, Watch now, and My List.
- Desktop uses cinematic artwork + poster composition; mobile becomes artwork-first with compact bottom copy.
- Removed homepage ticker, brand statement panel, partnership promo, and request/voting block.
- Simplified desktop and mobile navigation around Home, Browse, My List, Search/Profile.

CATALOG
- Trending is now presented as a compact Top 10 shelf.
- New & Updated is labeled New Release and retains freshness logic.
- Added up to six automatic genre shelves, ordered by catalog depth.
- Each genre shelf shows a compact horizontal row and View all action.
- All Drama remains the complete 30-title paginated catalog with compact genre filters.
- Poster cards are smaller, cleaner, denser, and optimized for scanning.

PERFORMANCE / PRODUCT
- Removed the initial community-poll fetch from the homepage because that module is no longer in the primary browsing flow.
- Preserves v23.8 player reliability, iOS handling, stale-load cancellation, source failover, and v23.7 catalog/source pagination.
- Service-worker shell bumped to v23.9 and homepage assets to v2390.

DEPLOYMENT
- Netlify frontend package only.
- No Supabase migration required.
- No Cloudflare change required.
